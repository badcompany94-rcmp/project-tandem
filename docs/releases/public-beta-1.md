# Project Tandem Public Beta 1

Project Tandem brings *Mario Kart: Double Dash!!* to native Windows with online solo/shared karts, natural widescreen, modern controllers, and separate menu and racing controls.

**You must supply your own compatible USA `GM4E01` ISO/CISO.** Setup verifies its executable revision and recompiles locally. These downloads do not contain a disc image, generated game source, or a compiled game.

## Choose your download

- **New player:** `Project-Tandem-Windows-Public-Beta-1.zip`. Extract completely, run `Setup-Tandem.cmd`, select your ISO, then run `Launch-Tandem.cmd`. Build tools are bundled.
- **Existing Alpha12 or newer portable install:** `Project-Tandem-Public-Beta-1-Cumulative-Update.zip`. Close the game, drag every ZIP file/folder over the existing installation, replace files, and keep `local-data`. Launch normally and allow the automatic rebuild if needed.
- GitHub's automatic source archives are for development, not the player installer.

Gameplay version: **Alpha25**. Compatibility ID: **`69b8a88ef96a4c0b`**. Everyone in a room needs a matching build. The public packaging keeps the tested Alpha25 gameplay code and preserves saves, controls, and display/sound settings.

## Included features

- Online rooms for 2–8 people, with individual and shared karts mixed in one event.
- Grand Prix with CPU fill, plus Balloon Battle, Shine Thief, and Bob-omb Blast with at least two opposing karts.
- Shared character markers, named reservations, duplicate-pick protection, and B/right-click reselection.
- Mouse/keyboard menu navigation and typing, switching automatically to dedicated racing controls. Race controls remain active through results and the ceremony and are rebindable in F1.
- Mouse/keyboard and controller interchangeability for player one, plus configurable devices and ports for offline multiplayer.
- Natural 16:9 widescreen, common resolution choices, windowed/fullscreen/borderless display, and volume settings starting at 25% on fresh installs.
- Persistent lobbies, seat changes, seven-character names, session medal stars, host removal/code refresh, and automatic online shutdown.
- Prediction/rollback, redundant peer datagrams, adaptive buffering, and EOS relay fallback, with an F10 FPS/RTT/BUF overlay.

The Alpha25 fixes include simultaneous shared-rider character claims, reselection synchronization, and a Grand Prix menu-memory failure after returning to a lobby.
