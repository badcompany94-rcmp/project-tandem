# Online Time Trials

Choose **Start Game > 1 Player > Online Time Trials**. Use Choose Course for a new run or Leaderboards to select a course and race a downloaded ghost. Mouse and controller navigation are supported. Selecting a leaderboard row goes through character/kart selection and straight to that ghost's course.

Keep Ghost enabled in the game's original Options menu. Only original courses and stock Time Trial rules are supported. Public boards and ghosts are separated by exact build ID, including when using different renderers.

After finishing, choose Retry, Change Name, To Leaderboards, Replay, Upload Ghost, or Quit. Upload is optional. The results screen does not expire. Change Name and F1 > Online edit the name used for the next upload, which accepts 1–7 letters or numbers.

The service stores the recording, your chosen display name, course, time, build ID and upload timestamp. Other players can download uploaded ghosts. Community times receive recording-format and integrity checks; they are not competition-certified and do not have authoritative anti-cheat verification.

The launcher creates a random local posting identity at `<user-data>/trials/identity.json`. Preserve it with your user-data backup. It is separate from EOS and is not an account or verified real-world identity. Do not share this file. The game does not upload your disc image or memory card.

The initial beta shows up to 50 times per course, allows up to 200 recordings per local identity, and has additional service-wide and network submission limits. A limit or connection error is displayed without blocking gameplay; wait and retry while the completed run is still available. Private development recordings are excluded from public boards.
